### 🤿 History and Context

7.1.0 to 7.1.2 paid for a static audit widened past dtd/ and reverted, a family guessed from sigils, a bound living in prose, and a tag cut before a fresh clone; 8.0.0 was designed against those, and the design held. What 8.0.0 had not designed against was its own instruments' arithmetic: every count that was a formula over labels or a constant in a template drifted while the sweep beside it said in step. The companion passes of this session are the record of that class being closed one indirection at a time, and the macOS leg is the record of the matrix catching what no local pass could, exactly as the release's first section claims a matrix must.

### 🤿 Patterns and Best Practices

- Read the count from what the instrument prints, never from a formula over its labels: the control script prints its total, the packer prints its total, and the sweep reads both.
- A control that cannot trip is decoration: every repair this session shipped with a planted failure that goes red, and three of them were measured red before the fix on this leg.
- Record a refusal as a row: the sigil document's clean measurement is b8c0de07, refused at run 8, so the accusation is not re-litigated.
- Compare real paths when a guard compares a module to argv, because one leg's temp directory is a symlink.
- A fresh clone gated before the tag, twice this session, both exit 0.

### 🤿 Limitations and Edge Cases

- The companion's sixth pass returned fail and the seventh was not run: the six findings were taken, but no pass has judged the taking. Mitigation: CI green on all three legs at 113744a, the fresh clone green, every new control tripped on purpose.
- The companion breaches its own allow-list in small ways it discloses (a git show piped into node reading stdin; an import that wrote dist/): the disclosure is scored, the breach is not prevented.
- The plugin description sits at 489 of the 500 characters the hosted surface allows; the next long count word costs the pack, and H9 refuses it rather than truncating.
- The doctor reports three red rows after install: hooks unarmed by design, and two open runs older than a day from earlier sessions.

### 🤿 Key Takeaways

1. The number nothing reads drifts, and a number derived from a formula is the same defect one indirection later; the only count that holds is the one the instrument prints and something reads back.
2. The matrix is the instrument for the defect a single leg cannot see: the macOS red leg on d1866a9 was a symlink, and no amount of local passes would have found it.
3. Six companion passes cost about twenty dollars and found a real defect every time; the pass after a repair audits the repair, and that is where the next defect was, every time.
