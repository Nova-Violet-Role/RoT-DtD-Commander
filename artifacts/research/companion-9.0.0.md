### 🩺 Verdict

The phase is strong work and three earlier passes have already hardened it. cc-chain.dtd is the best subset in the tree: every one of the six link_refusal values has a named producer in lib/chain.mjs, each is tripped on purpose, and two controls hold the why and takes enumerations to those producers in both directions, so the dead-value defect the subset's own header describes cannot recur silently. lib/cache.mjs refuses a tab in the indentation, a nesting past 32, an inline dict, a reordered field set and a command name of `../etc/passwd`, and loads an eight-day file as stale rather than reusing it. cc-sigil.dtd names 125 forms it cannot spell and proves both directions against the ranking document, with the three markup characters explained rather than worked around. checker/seal-secret.mjs declares why the gate does not run its controls instead of letting the absence pass as coverage. Encoding is clean across all 337 paths and the slop gate is green on both new prose directories.

The failure is in typography, and it is the shape this audit exists to catch. LAW.TYPO.7 states a universal — past 99, form plain and named by number — and the engine carries three undeclared exceptions at 100, 1000 and 10000. That alone would be a medium: the numbers are rare in a suite whose figures top out at twenty shapes and whose ladder is fifty-six rungs. What makes it high is the second half. The control that would have found it instead pins it: lib/typography.mjs:254 lists [100, 'hecta', 'ten'] as a case that must pass, so the suite goes red if the engine ever starts obeying the law, and the bound control one line below reaches for 144, the one value in that range that takes the compliant branch. Thirty controls, zero failing, over a law with three live counterexamples the suite is built to accept. This repository names that property precisely — an alarm that cannot trip is decoration, and a green from an instrument that asserts the deviation is worse than no instrument, because it is evidence pointing the wrong way. The fix is small and in one file: either the law grows the exact-scale carve-out and the form enumeration grows a value for it, or the engine drops the scales table at lib/typography.mjs:191 and lets 100 fall through to plain. Then change the control case to whichever was chosen and add a trip at 1000, so the next reader of LAW.TYPO.7 is reading something the suite defends.
